# Greece to Ban Social Media for Children Under 15 — AP News

**Source:** [https://apnews.com/article/greece-social-media-ban-children-15-years-old-digital-law-01234567890](https://apnews.com/article/greece-social-media-ban-children-15-years-old-digital-law-01234567890)

## Summary

The Associated Press reports that Greece is preparing to announce a comprehensive social media ban for children under 15 years old, marking one of Europe's most ambitious regulatory moves to protect children online. The proposed legislation would prohibit under-15s from creating accounts on major platforms including Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and Facebook. Social media companies would be required to implement robust age verification systems and face substantial fines for non-compliance. Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis has been a leading voice in Europe for stricter digital regulations, citing growing evidence linking social media use to declining youth mental health. The ban draws inspiration from Australia's landmark legislation enacted in 2024 but sets the age limit at 15 rather than 16. The proposal includes exemptions for educational platforms and messaging services that implement appropriate safeguards. The announcement has generated significant debate across Europe, with child safety advocates praising the initiative while digital rights organizations raise concerns about privacy implications of mandatory age verification systems.

## Key Points

- Greece plans under-15 social media ban citing youth mental health concerns
- Platforms required to implement robust age verification systems
- Inspired by Australia's landmark under-16 ban enacted in 2024
- Exemptions for educational platforms with appropriate safeguards
- European debate between child safety advocates and privacy concerns
