# Andy Harris Immigration and Foreign Workers Policy Debate - Politico

**Source:** [https://www.politico.com/news/2026/04/10/andy-harris-immigration-foreign-workers-00867621](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/04/10/andy-harris-immigration-foreign-workers-00867621)

## Summary

POLITICO examines Congressman Andy Harris's positions and legislative efforts concerning immigration policy and foreign worker programs, contributing to the ongoing contentious debate in US politics about skilled worker visas, particularly the H-1B visa program and related employment-based immigration categories. The article details Harris's legislative proposals regarding STEM workforce development, his stance on seasonal agricultural worker programs that affect his Maryland district's agricultural sector, and his broader approach to immigration enforcement versus labor market needs. The piece provides essential context on how these debates fit within larger questions about US economic competitiveness in technology, engineering, and specialized fields, the role of immigration in addressing critical labor shortages across multiple sectors of the American economy, and the deep political polarization surrounding immigration as a wedge issue in American electoral politics. The article also explores how restrictive immigration policies in major destination countries are reshaping global talent flows, creating both challenges and opportunities for countries of origin and alternative destination countries. These dynamics have significant implications for Turkey, which is simultaneously a significant source of highly skilled migrants who pursue education and career opportunities abroad, and an increasingly ambitious country seeking to attract and retain its own talent while also positioning itself as an emerging hub for technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship in the region. The analysis highlights how changing US immigration policies directly affect Turkish professionals, students, and researchers, as well as the broader bilateral economic relationship.
