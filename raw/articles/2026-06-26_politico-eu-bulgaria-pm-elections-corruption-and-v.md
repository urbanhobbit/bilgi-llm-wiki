# Politico EU: Bulgaria PM Elections, Corruption, and Vote Buying

**Source:** [https://www.politico.eu/article/bulgaria-pm-elections-corruption-vote-buying](https://www.politico.eu/article/bulgaria-pm-elections-corruption-vote-buying)

## Summary

Politico EU reports on Bulgaria's ongoing political crisis as the country heads to yet another election amid persistent corruption allegations and vote-buying scandals. The article examines how systemic corruption and entrenched political interests have undermined democratic governance in the EU member state, leading to a cycle of snap elections and unstable coalition governments. The piece analyzes the mechanisms of vote buying, the role of oligarchic networks in Bulgarian politics, and the EU's efforts to address rule-of-law deficiencies through the Cooperation and Verification Mechanism and other instruments. Bulgaria's case is presented as a cautionary tale about the limits of EU conditionality in addressing deeply embedded corruption and the challenges of democratic consolidation in post-communist EU member states.

## Key Points

- Bulgaria faces another snap election amid corruption and vote-buying scandals
- Systemic corruption undermines democratic institutions in EU member state
- EU conditionality mechanisms show limited effectiveness against embedded corruption
- Oligarchic networks continue to influence Bulgarian politics
- Political instability impacts regional cooperation and economic relations
