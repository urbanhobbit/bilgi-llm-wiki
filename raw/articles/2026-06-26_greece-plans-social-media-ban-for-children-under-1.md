# Greece Plans Social Media Ban for Children Under 15 (Reuters)

**Source:** [https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/greece-expected-announce-social-media-ban-children-under-15-2026-04-11/](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/greece-expected-announce-social-media-ban-children-under-15-2026-04-11/)

## Summary

This Reuters article reports on Greece's planned social media ban for children under 15, joining a global trend of age-based restrictions. The legislation would require age verification systems and restrict under-15 access without parental consent. Greece's initiative is part of broader European trend toward stricter regulation, following France, the UK, and Australia. The ban would affect major platforms including Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and Facebook. The article discusses enforcement challenges including age verification technology requirements, impacts on children's digital rights, and the balance between protection and participation. Critics argue bans may be less effective than digital literacy education combined with age-appropriate design standards like the UK's Children's Code.
