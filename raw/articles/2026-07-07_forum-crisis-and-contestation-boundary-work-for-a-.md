# Forum: Crisis and contestation – Boundary work for a future yet to come

**Author(s):** J. Speyer, Nils Stockmann, Esra Dilek, Lucrecia García Iommi, Özge Onursal-Beşgül, Hayley Walker, Hai Yang

**Published:** 2026

**Source:** [https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00108367261454268](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00108367261454268)

## Summary

The concepts of crisis and contestation have become ubiquitous in International Relations (IR) and beyond. Indeed, crisis and contestation are theoretical terms that crystallize the current challenges to global governance and international orders. This forum discusses the relationship and boundaries of these two concepts across a wide variety of empirical situations, illustrating how approaching a situation as a crisis or a contestation influences its understanding. The forum highlights four dimensions that structure the relationship and influence scholarly understanding of these challenges in a future yet to come. Published in Cooperation and Conflict (SAGE).

## Key Points

- Crisis and contestation are key concepts shaping current global governance challenges
- The relationship between these terms remains opaque despite their ubiquity in IR
- Forum brings together cross-disciplinary perspectives on crisis vs contestation
- Four dimensions structure the relationship between crisis and contestation
- Approach matters: framing a situation as crisis vs contestation changes how it is understood
- Includes Turkish scholars (Esra Dilek, Özge Onursal-Beşgül)
