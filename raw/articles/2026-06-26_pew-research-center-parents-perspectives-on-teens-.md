# Pew Research Center: Parents' Perspectives on Teens' AI Use — Probability-Based Survey Methodology

**Source:** [https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use)

## Summary

Pew Research Center's comprehensive survey examining parents' perspectives and attitudes regarding their teenage children's artificial intelligence use employs Pew's well-established and extensively documented survey research methodology with rigorous quality control procedures throughout the research process. The research utilizes Pew's American Trends Panel, a nationally representative probability-based online panel that is recruited through rigorous address-based sampling of residential addresses to ensure comprehensive coverage of the US population including households without internet access that are provided with connected devices. The survey methodology employs carefully calibrated sampling weights that are constructed through multi-stage adjustment processes to properly account for sampling frame coverage limitations and systematic nonresponse bias patterns, ensuring that survey estimates accurately represent the target population. The questionnaire design incorporates carefully validated measures of multiple constructs including general technology adoption patterns across household members, specific AI awareness metrics measuring familiarity with different AI applications, usage patterns examining how teens actually engage with AI tools in practice, parental mediation strategies including rule-setting, co-use, and active mediation, and comprehensive privacy concerns regarding data collection and use by AI systems. Methodological strengths that characterize Pew's approach include the organization's longstanding practice of transparent reporting of essential survey design parameters including sample sizes, response rates calculated according to standard definitions, and detailed weighting procedures, enabling informed assessment of data quality and appropriate interpretation of findings. The analytical framework includes comprehensive descriptive population estimates with confidence intervals, systematic subgroup comparisons across demographic and socioeconomic categories, and logistic regression modeling to identify factors independently associated with parental attitudes while controlling for potential confounders. The survey demonstrates methodological awareness of emerging challenges in researching AI-related attitudes including the significant variation in public understanding and mental models of AI technologies, the rapid and continuing evolution of AI tools and applications accessible to teenagers, and the inherent difficulty of measuring nuanced privacy and safety concerns that may be unfamiliar or abstract to respondents. This research provides methodologically rigorous baseline data on parental perspectives that substantively informs both academic understanding of family technology dynamics and evidence-based policy development regarding youth engagement with increasingly sophisticated AI systems and applications.

## Key Points

- Pew's American Trends Panel provides a rigorously constructed probability-based sampling infrastructure for nationally representative online survey research
- Multi-stage calibrated sampling weights adjust for sampling frame coverage limitations and systematic nonresponse bias, ensuring accurate population estimates
- Validated measures of technology adoption patterns, AI awareness and usage, parental mediation strategies, and privacy concerns enable systematic attitude measurement
- Logistic regression modeling identifies demographic, socioeconomic, and experiential predictors of parental perspectives on teenage AI use
- Transparent reporting of all survey design parameters, response rates, weighting procedures, and methodological limitations maintains research credibility and enables informed interpretation
