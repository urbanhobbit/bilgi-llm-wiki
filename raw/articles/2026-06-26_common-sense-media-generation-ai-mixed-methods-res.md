# Common Sense Media: Generation AI — Mixed-Methods Research on Children and Artificial Intelligence

**Source:** [https://www.commonsensemedia.org/sites/default/files/research/report/commonsensemedia_generationai.pdf](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/sites/default/files/research/report/commonsensemedia_generationai.pdf)

## Summary

Common Sense Media's 'Generation AI' research report provides a methodologically rigorous and comprehensive examination of children's engagement with artificial intelligence technologies in their everyday lives. The research employs a sophisticated mixed-methods design combining a large-scale nationally representative survey of children aged 8-18 and their parents with in-depth qualitative focus groups designed to capture rich experiential narratives around AI interaction. The survey methodology utilizes validated and age-appropriate measurement scales for assessing multiple dimensions including AI literacy and conceptual understanding, perceived AI trustworthiness and reliability, attitudes toward AI integration in educational contexts, privacy concerns regarding AI data collection, and social and emotional responses to AI interaction. The sampling strategy employs probability-based panel recruitment with careful demographic stratification to ensure robust representativeness across age groups spanning childhood through adolescence, socioeconomic diversity, geographic variation across urban, suburban, and rural settings, and varying levels of prior AI exposure and familiarity. The qualitative component employs rigorous thematic analysis of focus group transcripts using established grounded theory approaches including open coding, axial coding, and selective coding to systematically capture emergent themes in children's own understanding, expectations, concerns, and experiences with AI technologies. The methodological framework thoughtfully addresses key challenges inherent in researching children's AI engagement including the rapid and ongoing evolution of accessible AI tools, varying levels of AI awareness and conceptual sophistication among children at different developmental stages, and the complex ethical considerations of conducting research with minors about emerging and potentially sensitive technologies. The analytical approach appropriately includes comprehensive descriptive statistics establishing baseline patterns, regression models examining predictors of AI-related attitudes and behaviors across demographic and experiential variables, and systematic qualitative theme frequency and co-occurrence analysis. This report makes a foundational contribution to the emerging methodological literature on researching children's interactions with AI systems, establishing validated measurement approaches and ethical research protocols for this rapidly evolving and socially consequential field of inquiry.

## Key Points

- Mixed-methods design combining large-scale nationally representative survey with in-depth qualitative focus groups comprehensively captures children's AI experiences
- Validated and age-appropriate scales for AI literacy, perceived trustworthiness, attitudes, privacy concerns, and emotional responses enable systematic measurement
- Probability-based panel sampling with demographic stratification ensures robust representativeness across age groups, socioeconomic backgrounds, and geographic regions
- Qualitative thematic analysis using grounded theory approaches systematically captures emergent themes in children's own AI understanding and concerns
- Age-appropriate ethical protocols and participatory research methods are essential when conducting research with minors about emerging AI technologies
