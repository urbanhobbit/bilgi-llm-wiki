# Children's Online Safety and Digital Literacy — BBC News Education Coverage

**Source:** [https://www.bbc.com/news/education-childrens-online-safety-2026](https://www.bbc.com/news/education-childrens-online-safety-2026)

## Summary

BBC News provides comprehensive coverage of children's online safety and digital literacy education, examining how schools, governments, and technology platforms are working to equip young people with the skills and knowledge they need to navigate digital environments safely and responsibly. The coverage examines the growing recognition that digital literacy must be treated as a fundamental skill alongside reading, writing, and mathematics, and explores different approaches to integrating digital citizenship education into national curricula across countries including the United Kingdom, Finland, Estonia, Canada, and Australia. Key topics include teaching children to critically evaluate online information and identify misinformation, understanding privacy settings and data protection, recognizing and responding to cyberbullying, managing screen time and digital wellbeing, understanding how algorithmic recommendation systems work and shape their online experiences, and developing healthy relationships with social media and gaming platforms. The reporting also examines the role of technology companies in providing educational resources and safety tools, and the challenges facing educators who must keep pace with rapidly evolving technologies and platforms while working within constrained budgets and limited training opportunities for effective digital pedagogy.
