## Summary

This Guardian investigation reveals that AI-generated child sexual abuse material has surged dramatically online, with the Internet Watch Foundation reporting unprecedented volumes of synthetic abuse content created using generative AI tools. The article documents how these tools create photorealistic depictions that challenge existing detection systems, with tech companies scrambling to develop new AI-powered countermeasures. The scale of the problem continues to outpace mitigation efforts despite cross-industry collaboration. The UK Online Safety Act and EU Digital Services Act are highlighted as potential regulatory models for holding platforms accountable for failing to detect and remove AI-generated abuse content. The article calls for urgent investment in automated detection technologies and updated legal frameworks.
