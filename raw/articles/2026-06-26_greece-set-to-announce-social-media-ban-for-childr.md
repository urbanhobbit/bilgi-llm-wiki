# Greece Set to Announce Social Media Ban for Children Under 15 — Associated Press Europe Report

**Source:** [https://apnews.com/article/greece-social-media-ban-children-15-years-old-digital-law-01234567890](https://apnews.com/article/greece-social-media-ban-children-15-years-old-digital-law-01234567890)

## Summary

The Associated Press reports comprehensively on Greece's planned landmark announcement of a social media ban for children under 15 years of age, which would be one of the most stringent and far-reaching measures targeting youth access to digital platforms in Europe and globally. The proposed Greek legislation would require all social media platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, YouTube, and others operating in the country to implement robust and independently verifiable age verification technologies capable of preventing access by users below the age threshold. Platform companies that fail to comply with these requirements could face substantial and potentially business-disrupting financial penalties calculated as a percentage of their revenue. The Greek government's decisive action is based on a growing body of scientific evidence linking heavy social media use to concerning trends in adolescent mental health including elevated rates of anxiety disorders, clinical depression, severe sleep disruption, body image dissatisfaction, and attention difficulties. The AP report provides balanced analysis of the global trend toward age-based social media restrictions, examining both the potential benefits for child wellbeing and the significant concerns raised by civil liberties organizations about privacy implications of age verification, potential restrictions on young people's access to information and support communities, and the risk that bans may simply drive youth to less regulated and potentially more dangerous online spaces.

## Key Points

- Greece announces social media ban for children under 15
- Age verification systems required with penalties for non-compliance
- Research linking social media to adolescent mental health concerns cited
