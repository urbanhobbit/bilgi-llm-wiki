# Politico EU: Germany's Nuclear Debate - Is Nuclear Over?

**Source:** [https://www.politico.eu/article/germany-berlin-nuclear-is-over-the-debate-isnt](https://www.politico.eu/article/germany-berlin-nuclear-is-over-the-debate-isnt)

## Summary

Politico EU examines Germany's ongoing debate about nuclear energy following its complete phase-out in 2023. The article explores whether the nuclear chapter is definitively closed for Germany or whether energy security concerns and climate goals might revive the discussion. The piece analyzes the political, economic, and technical dimensions of Germany's nuclear exit, including the role of the Greens in shaping energy policy, the economics of nuclear compared to renewables, and the implications for Germany's energy security following the loss of Russian gas supplies. The debate is set against the backdrop of European energy diversification efforts and the EU's taxonomy that classifies nuclear as a sustainable investment under certain conditions, creating tension between German and French approaches to low-carbon energy policy.

## Key Points

- Germany's nuclear phase-out faces renewed scrutiny amid energy security concerns
- Political dynamics including Green party influence shape German energy policy
- EU taxonomy classifying nuclear as sustainable creates intra-EU tensions
- Germany-France nuclear policy divergence impacts EU energy strategy
- Economic comparison between nuclear and renewables informs policy debate
