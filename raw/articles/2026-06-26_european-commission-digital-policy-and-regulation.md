# European Commission: Digital Policy and Regulation

**Source:** [https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1234](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1234)

## Summary

The European Commission's digital policy page outlines the EU's comprehensive approach to digital regulation, including the Digital Services Act, Digital Markets Act, AI Act, and related legislation. These regulations establish a unified digital governance framework across the EU single market, addressing content moderation, platform accountability, competition in digital markets, and the governance of artificial intelligence. The Commission's approach balances innovation promotion with fundamental rights protection, setting standards that influence regulatory debates worldwide.

## Key Points

- EU comprehensive digital regulatory framework shapes global standards
- Digital Services Act and AI Act represent landmark regulations
- EU approach balances innovation with fundamental rights protection
- Single market regulation creates unified framework across member states
- EU digital standards influence regulatory debates worldwide
