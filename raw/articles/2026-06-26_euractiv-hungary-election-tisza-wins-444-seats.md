# Euractiv: Hungary Election - Tisza Wins 444 Seats

**Source:** [https://www.euractiv.com/section/europe/politics/hungary-election-tisza-wins-444-seats](https://www.euractiv.com/section/europe/politics/hungary-election-tisza-wins-444-seats)

## Summary

Euractiv reports on the 2026 Hungarian election results, with the Tisza Party winning 444 seats in parliament. The article examines the scale of Viktor Orbán's defeat and the implications for EU-Hungary relations. The supermajority victory gives the Tisza Party and Péter Magyar a strong mandate for reform, including potential restoration of rule-of-law standards, media freedom, and judicial independence. The article analyzes how this election outcome will affect EU decision-making on key issues including Ukraine aid, sanctions against Russia, and the EU budget.

## Key Points

- Tisza Party wins 444 seats in Hungarian parliamentary election
- Super-majority provides strong mandate for democratic reform
- Potential restoration of rule-of-law, media freedom, judicial independence
- EU-Hungary relations expected to transform significantly
- Impact on EU decision-making on Ukraine and Russia sanctions
