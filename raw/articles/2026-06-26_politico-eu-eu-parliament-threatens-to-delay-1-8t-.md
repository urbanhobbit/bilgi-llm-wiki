# Politico EU: EU Parliament Threatens to Delay €1.8T Budget Talks Until 2027

**Source:** [https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-parliament-threatens-to-delay-e1-8t-budget-talks-until-2027](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-parliament-threatens-to-delay-e1-8t-budget-talks-until-2027)

## Summary

Politico EU reports on the European Parliament's threat to delay negotiations on the EU's next long-term budget (the Multiannual Financial Framework) until 2027. The €1.8 trillion budget framework is facing political gridlock as member states disagree on priorities, funding sources, and conditionality mechanisms. The article examines the political dynamics behind the budget impasse, including divisions between net contributor and net beneficiary countries, disagreements over rule-of-law conditionality, and the challenge of funding new priorities like defense, climate transition, and digital transformation within existing fiscal constraints. The delay threatens to impact EU programs ranging from agricultural subsidies to research funding and regional development. The article also explores the implications of the budget delay for the EU's strategic autonomy ambitions and its ability to respond to external challenges including the war in Ukraine and US-China strategic competition.

## Key Points

- EU Parliament threatens delay of €1.8 trillion budget talks
- Member state divisions over priorities and conditionality create gridlock
- Budget impasse impacts defense, climate, and digital funding priorities
- Rule-of-law conditionality remains contentious issue in budget negotiations
- Delay challenges EU's strategic autonomy and crisis response capacity
