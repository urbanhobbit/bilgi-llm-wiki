# Quantitative, Qualitative, and Mixed Methodologies in Digital Social Sciences (OA Book)

**Author(s):** Łukasz Sułkowski, Robert Seliga, Zdzisława Dacko-Pikiewicz

**Published:** 2026-07-07

**Source:** [https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/oa-mono/10.4324/9781003790297/quantitative-qualitative-mixed-methodologies-digital-social-sciences-%C5%82ukasz-su%C5%82kowski-zdzis%C5%82awa-dacko-pikiewicz-robert-seliga](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/oa-mono/10.4324/9781003790297/quantitative-qualitative-mixed-methodologies-digital-social-sciences-%C5%82ukasz-su%C5%82kowski-zdzis%C5%82awa-dacko-pikiewicz-robert-seliga)

## Summary

Open Access book published by Routledge (Taylor & Francis) with funding from the European Commission, bringing together quantitative analysis, qualitative inquiry, and mixed methods for the digital era. The volume shows how to design robust social science research using online surveys, platform data, digital traces, and computational tools, while keeping theory, context, and interpretation in view. Designed for doctoral students and early-career researchers across sociology, psychology, education, public policy, communication, and management. Chapters cover: Quantitative Research in Social Sciences, Qualitative Research in Social Sciences, Mixed-Methods Research, Digital Methods and Tools, Big Data and Machine Learning in Social Sciences, Ethics in Digital Social Science Research, and Integration.

## Key Points

- Open Access (CC BY), funded by European Commission
- Bridges quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods for digital social sciences
- Covers online surveys, platform data, digital traces, and computational tools
- Includes chapters on Big Data and ML in social sciences
- Addresses ethics in digital social science research
- Practical methodology guide for doctoral students and researchers
