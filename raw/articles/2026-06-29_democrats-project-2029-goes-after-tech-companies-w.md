# Democrats' 'Project 2029' goes after tech companies with online safety plan

**Author(s):** Nicholas Wu

**Published:** 2026-06-29

**Source:** [https://www.semafor.com/article/06/28/2026/democrats-project-2029-goes-after-tech-companies-with-online-safety-plan](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/28/2026/democrats-project-2029-goes-after-tech-companies-with-online-safety-plan)

## Summary

Democrats are rolling out 'Project 2029', a policy framework for the 2028 presidential race that starts with online child safety. The 'Kids Over Clicks' proposal calls for narrowing Section 230 protections, banning social media accounts for kids under 16, banning cell phones in schools, promoting smartphone-free childhood until age 14, banning surveillance advertising, and limiting data collection on children. The group, formed as a counterweight to conservative Project 2025, chose child safety as its first focus because it is among the least politically polarizing topics. The proposal is supported by Jonathan Haidt (who calls it social media's 'tobacco moment'), Sen. Cory Booker, New Jersey Gov. Mikie Sherrill, and the American Federation of Teachers.

## Key Points

- Project 2029 is Democrats' counterweight to conservative Project 2025
- 'Kids Over Clicks' proposal targets Section 230 reform for child safety
- Calls for social media ban for under-16, phone-free schools, smartphone-free childhood until 14
- Ban on surveillance advertising and limited children's data collection
- Designed partly for executive action without congressional approval
- Jonathan Haidt calls it social media's 'tobacco moment'
