# Protecting Children's Data Privacy — UK Information Commissioner's Office Guidance for Organizations

**Source:** [https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/kids-protecting-childrens-data](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/kids-protecting-childrens-data)

## Summary

The UK Information Commissioner's Office provides detailed and authoritative guidance for organizations on how to protect children's data privacy in compliance with the UK GDPR and the Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code). The guidance explains that any online service likely to be accessed by children must embed data protection by design and by default into every aspect of their service, from initial concept through deployment and ongoing operation. Key requirements include conducting thorough Data Protection Impact Assessments specifically focused on children's needs and vulnerabilities, implementing the highest privacy settings as the default configuration for child users, providing clear and age-appropriate information about how children's data is collected, used, and shared, ensuring that children's best interests are a primary consideration in all design and development decisions, minimizing data collection to only what is strictly necessary, restricting profiling and automated decision-making, and providing accessible tools for children and parents to exercise their data rights.

## Key Points

- ICO provides comprehensive guidance on UK GDPR and Children's Code compliance
- Data Protection Impact Assessments mandatory for children-facing services
- Default privacy settings and age-appropriate information required by law
