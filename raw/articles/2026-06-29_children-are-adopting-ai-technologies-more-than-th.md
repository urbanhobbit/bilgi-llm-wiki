# Children are adopting AI technologies more than three times faster than adults — UNICEF Statement

**Author(s):** UNICEF

**Published:** 2026-06-29

**Source:** [https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/children-are-adopting-ai-technologies-more-three-times-faster-adults](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/children-are-adopting-ai-technologies-more-three-times-faster-adults)

## Summary

UNICEF press release ahead of the first Global Dialogue on AI Governance reveals that at least 20 million children across 10 countries have used AI, adopting it at rates more than three times faster than adults. More than 2 million children (1 in 10) turn to AI for advice on things that worry them, and an estimated 13 million use it to support learning and homework. A third of children reported concerns about AI being used to scam and trick others or spread misinformation, while a quarter feared having their images or videos manipulated into sexually explicit deepfakes. UNICEF is calling on governments and the private sector to embed child rights in global AI governance. The data draws from Disrupting Harm Phase 2 research led by UNICEF Innocenti, ECPAT International, and INTERPOL, surveying nationally representative samples of internet-using children aged 12-17 across Armenia, Brazil, Colombia, Dominican Republic, Jordan, Mexico, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Pakistan, and Serbia.

## Key Points

- 20M+ children have used AI across 10 countries surveyed
- Children adopt AI 3x faster than adults
- 2M+ children (1 in 10) use AI for personal advice on worries
- 13M children use AI for learning and homework support
- 1/3 of children fear AI scams and misinformation
- 1/4 fear AI-manipulated deepfake sexual content
- UNICEF calls for child rights embedded in global AI governance
- First Global Dialogue on AI Governance cited as milestone
