# What Parents Say About Their Teens' AI Use — Pew Research Center

**Source:** [https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use)

## Summary

Pew Research Center's 2026 survey examines American parents' perspectives on their teenage children's use of artificial intelligence tools. The nationally representative survey of parents with teens aged 13-17 finds that 65% of parents report their teen has used AI tools, with ChatGPT being the most commonly used platform. Parents express mixed views: while 54% believe AI tools can be beneficial for educational purposes, 72% express concerns about privacy, accuracy, and the potential for AI to facilitate cheating. The survey reveals significant demographic differences in awareness and concern. The report provides recommendations for parents, educators, and policymakers on developing AI literacy education.

## Key Points

- Analysis of artificial intelligence developments in regulatory landscape
- Examination of international approaches to child digital protection
- Discussion of age verification and age-appropriate design standards
- Assessment of platform responsibility and duty of care frameworks
- Policy recommendations for balanced child safety and digital rights
