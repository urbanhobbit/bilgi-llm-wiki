# What parents say about their teen's AI use | Pew Research Center

**Source:** [https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use/](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/what-parents-say-about-their-teens-ai-use/)

## Summary

This Pew Research Center report published February 24, 2026, by researchers Colleen McClain, Monica Anderson, Olivia Sidoti, and William Bishop, is part of a landmark study titled 'How Teens Use and View AI.' The study comprehensively explores the landscape of teens and technology with a specific focus on artificial intelligence adoption and usage patterns. It examines how teens use AI chatbots for schoolwork including essay drafting, research assistance, and problem-solving, their confidence in AI-generated information versus traditional sources, and their nuanced views on AI's future societal impact. The specific section on parents reveals significant and potentially consequential gaps between parent perceptions and actual teen behavior regarding AI use. The research employs rigorous survey methodology based on a nationally representative sample and provides detailed demographic breakdowns of AI usage patterns among teenagers aged 13 to 17 across various socioeconomic and geographic categories. Key findings indicate that many parents substantially underestimate both the frequency and diversity of their teens' AI interactions, which range from academic assistance to emotional support, creative projects, and personal decision-making. The study also explores teens' critical evaluation skills regarding AI outputs and their sophisticated understanding of AI's limitations and potential biases. The report includes detailed appendix tables, comprehensive methodology descriptions explaining sampling and weighting procedures, and contextualizes the complex relationship between teens' digital lives and parental awareness.

## Key Points

- Pew Research Center study on teens' AI use published Feb 24, 2026
- Reveals significant gap between parent perceptions and teen AI behavior
- Covers AI chatbot use for schoolwork, emotional support, creative projects
- Survey-based methodology with demographic breakdowns
- Examines teens' critical evaluation of AI outputs
- Includes detailed appendix tables and methodology descriptions
